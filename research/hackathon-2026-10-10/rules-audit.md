## Submission package

The [overview requirements](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/) require:

1. A functioning coding/developer-tool project using at least one NVIDIA open model on Nebius Token Factory or AI Cloud.
2. A selected track and description covering purpose, implementation, and behavior.
3. A URL for an operational demo, hosted app, or test build. Coding submissions have no Physical AI exemption.
4. A publicly visible YouTube demonstration, at most three minutes, showing operation and Nebius/NVIDIA use.
5. A public GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository with a discoverable open-source license, setup/run README, and clear explanation of model and Nebius usage.
6. Feedback about the Nebius/NVIDIA technologies used.
7. For an earlier project, a written account of substantial improvements made during the submission window. Existing work is allowed; merely having an older repository is not disqualifying.
8. For the city award, the relevant attended event city.

Organizer guidance additionally recommends identifying the technologies audibly in the video and in the project description and Built With fields; specific feedback should discuss actual use, onboarding, strengths, problems, and willingness to reuse. AI coding assistants are expressly encouraged. These are [submission tips](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/updates/46205-how-to-build-a-winning-project), not evidence that generated code functions.

## Platform and access

The [FAQ](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/faqs) confirms that an NVIDIA-model runtime call through Token Factory satisfies the platform condition; unrelated frontend/backend hosting can be elsewhere. Another inference provider alone does not substitute. Country onboarding problems are separate from contest eligibility, with no approved region workaround. Missing credits/access are handled by Nebius case by case. Teams have no published maximum size. Promotional grants are Token Factory credits, with no separate published AI Cloud grant.

Implementation judgment: place a live NVIDIA call through Token Factory on the product's actual coding path, retain inspectable usage evidence, and demonstrate resulting edits/tests. A scripted fixture or an unrelated decorative call would make the entry weak even if its infrastructure is sophisticated.

## Additional binding constraints

[Rules §§3–8](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules), condensed:

- Entrants: adults under residence law; eligible teams/organizations need an authorized representative. Exclusions include Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, sanctioned/prohibited jurisdictions; organizers, involved personnel, specified families/households, judges/employers, affiliates, and conflicts.
- Register and complete required fields. Supply necessary source/assets/instructions; ensure installation and behavior match claims. English or translations are required. Video rights require permission; target under three minutes.
- Maintain unrestricted, free judging access until judging ends; provide credentials if private. Judges may rely entirely on submission media.
- Own the contribution; honor third-party licenses and permissions; enhance incorporated open-source work. Sponsor/administrator-funded or preferentially supported development is excluded.
- After cutoff, submission changes need narrow organizer permission. Multiple entries must differ substantially.
- Tie-break order: implementation, design, impact, idea; remaining ties use judge voting. Feedback scoring considers completeness, viability, impact.
- Overall awards accept all eligible entries; Tavily eligibility requires an actual runtime API call.

Unresolved interpretation: “significant” improvement has no numerical threshold. Ordinary promotional-credit eligibility alongside the support exclusion is not explicitly reconciled.

## Judging and prizes

The [judging update](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/updates/46204-here-s-how-judging-works) specifies an initial viability/theme pass-or-fail gate, then scores of 1–5 for four equally weighted dimensions. Equal weighting implies **25% each**: technical implementation, product design, demonstrated impact, and originality/problem understanding. It specifically favors coding agents that plan, edit, test, and iterate on an actual repository with little intervention. No special separate coding-track weighting is published there.

The [prize update](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/updates/46206-did-someone-say-prizes) distinguishes:

| Award | Published prize |
|---|---|
| Overall first / second / third | $20,000 / $10,000 / $6,000 |
| Coding and Agentic Engineering winner | One NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano |
| Each other track winner | One NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano |
| Tavily bonus | $3,000 |
| City awards | 20 × $500 |
| Feedback awards | 10 × $100 plus NVIDIA swag |

The update permits one overall award **or** one track award plus one bonus award. Do not budget an overall award plus bonuses. These are competitive awards, not participation grants.

## Credits and ambiguities

[Resources](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/resources) advertises $25 Token Factory credit through the linked promotional form using `NEBIUS-DEVPOST-GLOBAL26`, plus another $25 through the Builders Program. It also suggests extra event benefits, without a guaranteed allocation. The [Builders Program](https://dev.nebius.com/builders) requires signup and email verification to claim benefits; it lists $25 Token Factory and $25 Tavily credits, with partner offers subject to eligibility, availability, and their own terms. Delivery, expiration, and card-free access are not established by these pages. **Access uncertainty:** the [demo-availability discussion](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/forum_topics/45448-does-the-demo-need-to-stay-live-between-submission-and-judging) contains participant interpretation, not an organizer exemption. Neither relaxes the rules. Test actual onboarding before depending on credits; budget continued judging access.
